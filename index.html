Visit fameh.gemini50.com ↗
The problem

Resolution 3100 imposes complex compliance requirements that many independent physicians struggle to understand and manage. Outdated documentation, poor information control, and limited administrative resources put their service authorization (habilitación) at risk.

While large healthcare organizations have the resources to manage compliance, independent providers often lack the time, staff, and infrastructure. FAMEH bridges this gap with an agile, secure, and affordable solution that simplifies regulatory compliance through technology.

Discovery & research

I ran discovery with the three groups that live with the regulation every day.

PhysiciansAuditorsAdministrative staff InterviewsSurveysAI-assisted market research

I used AI tools to support market research, such as the average rate of habilitation losses and the total number of providers in the country, and to document the interviews.

Key findings

Manual operation is inefficient

Habilitation is managed with scattered, manual processes.

Non-compliance is high

Many providers do not meet the requirements of the regulation.

The regulation is not well understood

Providers lack clear knowledge of what Resolution 3100 asks of them.

Oversight is hard for regulators

Control bodies struggle to supervise the status of every habilitation granted in the country.

Prioritization

To define the MVP scope, I treated the regulation itself as the backlog and ranked it by risk.

  1. Map the regulationIdentify every regulatory requirement in Resolution 3100.
  2. Assess impactRate each requirement's impact through a risk analysis.
  3. Prioritize with MoSCoWSort requirements into Must, Should, Could and Won't for the MVP.
MustMVP scope
ShouldNext in line
CouldLater releases
Won'tOut for now
Building with AI
  • Discovery: AI tools supported market research and interview documentation.
  • Prototype as validation: I built the full prototype with Claude Design and used it to validate the project with users.
  • Delivery: the whole team works with AI for user stories, estimates, code generation and testing.
Results

The three pilots had strong acceptance from all three user groups: physicians, auditors and administrative staff.
